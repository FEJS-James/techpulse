## Backlink Strategy
AI articles link to https://commercialcoding.com (ONLY AI articles — not gaming/hardware)
Position naturally:
- OpenClaw/agent articles → "Need AI agent setup? Commercial Coding builds custom AI workflows"
- AI tool reviews → "Commercial Coding helps businesses deploy these tools"
- LLM/automation articles → "Commercial Coding integrates AI into business operations"
Non-AI articles: NO commercialcoding.com backlink

## Content Guidelines
- 1,500-2,500 words per article
- SEO-optimized titles with current year
- Include comparison tables where relevant
- Link to sources but add original analysis
- Be opinionated — readers want takes, not press releases
- Always include the commercialcoding.com backlink naturally
